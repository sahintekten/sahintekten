# Hi, I'm Sahin

Final-year Computer Science student at University College Dublin, graduating in May 2027. I build things people use, mostly AI agents and the backend around them.

**What I'm working on**

- **[SEDA](https://withseda.com)** – I'm the founder. SEDA is an AI receptionist for aesthetic and hair transplant clinics. It answers enquiries on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger and books consultations. It has been live with a clinic in Istanbul since July 2026, with around 300 agent runs a day, a ~20 s median reply time and 99.2% accuracy on price answers in audits. The chat routing part is open source: [seda-chat-router](https://github.com/sahintekten/seda-chat-router).
- **Final year project** – a research prototype that helps people practise cognitive restructuring through conversation, with its own safety layer.
